About Us

Who We Are

We are a team of students from the IT 362 Frontend Web Development class at Asia-Pacific International University. This site was created as a class project to apply practical web development skills while researching and showcasing Thailand’s rich cultural heritage. Our mission is to connect people, preserve traditions, and share stories that matter.

Our Mission

Our mission is to preserve, celebrate, and promote the unique cultural heritage of Thailand by creating engaging, educational, and accessible experiences for both locals and visitors. We work closely with artisans, community leaders, scholars, and cultural institutions to document traditions, support living practices, and ensure that cultural knowledge is recorded and shared responsibly.

Through storytelling, guided visits, multimedia content, and capacity building initiatives, we aim to create sustainable opportunities for local communities while making cultural learning welcoming and meaningful for audiences of all ages and backgrounds.

Our Vision

We envision a future where Thailand’s heritage is celebrated as a living resource that enriches local life and connects people across the world. In this future, cultural tourism supports resilient local economies, fosters intergenerational knowledge transfer, and encourages environmental and social sustainability.

By combining respectful stewardship with modern digital tools and collaborative partnerships, we hope to make cultural traditions more visible and valued, so they continue to inspire creativity, pride, and shared responsibility for generations to come.

Meet Our Team

Our Story

What began as a small, grassroots effort has grown into a vibrant cultural preservation project driven by curiosity, respect, and collaboration. Initially developed as part of the IT 362 Frontend Web Development class at Asia-Pacific International University, this project gave students a chance to combine research with practical web design and development. Early on, volunteers and local researchers started collecting oral histories, photographing traditional practices, and mapping lesser-known cultural sites. Their work revealed deep, living traditions that deserved wider recognition and thoughtful support.

Over time we partnered with historians, artisans, community leaders, and educators to expand those efforts into community programs, workshops, and visitor experiences that prioritize local voices. By combining field research with digital storytelling and ethical tourism development, we aim to create opportunities that benefit both cultural bearers and curious visitors.

Today, our project is a living network of people who care about safeguarding traditions while making them accessible in modern contexts. We continue to learn from the communities we work with, and we measure success by the resilience of cultural practices, the well-being of participating communities, and the meaningful connections formed between people and place.
